## Post 1 — The design principle

Building **Thesis** for the Alpaca AI Trading Agents Hackathon.

The rule: an AI options agent should not risk a dollar until it can state:

1. Why the trade exists  
2. When it is wrong  
3. The maximum loss

Grok proposes. Deterministic gates prove. Alpaca paper infrastructure executes.

## Post 2 — The failure that improved the product

Our first dashboard sometimes rendered blank because live broker reads blocked the
page before first paint.

We replaced it with a custom read-only FastAPI audit console: immediate shell,
background Alpaca reconciliation, explicit loading/stale/error states, and no
broker-write endpoints.

The setback became the strongest part of the judge experience.
